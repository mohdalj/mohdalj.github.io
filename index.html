Senior Design Project · 2026–now Team of five

VR safety training for construction sites

"Development of a VR Training Module to Reduce Mobile-Equipment Accident via Traffic Management Plans"

  • Mobile equipment
  • Floor openings
  • Falling objects
  • Moving machinery
  • Rotating equipment

Five high-risk scenarios. Each one shows the unsafe condition, explains the hazard, then shows the safe way to work. It runs on head-mounted VR or a VR igloo, for one learner or a group. My part was the input-process-output (IPO) flow, the assumptions and the Gantt chart. I also wrote IEEE-format decision matrices to justify design choices, and kept the logbook and report sections.

SimLab Composer · IPO · Gantt · Decision matrices

OPEX 411 · DMAIC · 2026 Group project

Campus parking access improvement

I wrote the Introduction and the Define phase of the DMAIC report. That meant mapping the whole parking-access process with a SIPOC diagram (suppliers, inputs, process, outputs, customers) so the team agreed on scope before anyone measured anything.

DMAIC · SIPOC · Process mapping
